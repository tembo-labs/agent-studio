# Blog Post: Tembo Agent Studio v0.3 - Governance as a Product Feature

v0.3 is about operational trust.

By this phase, teams can already deploy TAS and author changes through chat-driven PR workflows. The next requirement is predictable governance: clear accountability, strong human-in-the-loop controls, and reliable observability.

## Why Governance Needed Its Own Milestone
Many automation products treat governance as a later add-on. We view it as a core product function.

If teams cannot answer who changed behavior, when it changed, and why it changed, they cannot safely scale usage.

## What v0.3 Adds
- Immutable changelog records around `who`, `when`, and `why`.
- Rich human forms for approvals and structured intervention.
- Per-agent operational dashboard views for runs, history, and active human tasks.
- Stronger policy controls for administrators.

## Operational Impact
This phase improves day-two operations:
- support teams can troubleshoot with context,
- compliance teams can audit change history,
- admins can enforce guardrails without blocking all progress.

## Foundation for v0.4
Adaptive intelligence (correction-to-code, variants, and shared learning) only works when governance is already robust.

v0.3 creates that baseline.
